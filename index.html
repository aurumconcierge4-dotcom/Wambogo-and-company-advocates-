A firm built to stay close to the matter.

Wambogo & Company Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm practising from Hill Plaza, Upper Hill, advising individuals, businesses and institutions across Kenya.

We keep caseloads deliberately limited. Every matter is handled directly by an advocate who knows its full history, not passed between juniors as it moves through stages — a structure we've kept as the firm has grown, because it's the one that produces better outcomes for clients.

Our advocates appear before the High Court, the Employment and Labour Relations Court, and the Magistrates' Courts, and advise on commercial, constitutional and property matters outside the courtroom.

02 — Our Practice

Five disciplines. One standard.

Commercial Law
Advisory and representation across commercial disputes, contracts, negotiations, arbitration and corporate affairs. We act for parties at every stage — structuring the transaction, resolving the dispute, or enforcing the obligation — with attention fixed on the commercial objective behind the legal problem.
Employment & Industrial Relations
Employment relationships are governed by contracts, statutes and court decisions — and most disputes turn on what was documented, when, and by whom. We advise on employment contracts, workplace disputes, termination, statutory compliance, and represent clients before the Employment and Labour Relations Court.
Constitutional & Human Rights
Where rights are engaged — by state action or by private conduct — we provide representation and advisory work across constitutional rights, fair administrative action, fair labour practices and related public-law matters.
Criminal Law
A criminal charge requires immediate, focused defence. We represent clients in proceedings before the Magistrates' Courts and the High Court, from the earliest stages through conclusion, together with related advisory work.
Conveyancing, Real Estate & Property
Property transactions carry lasting consequences — a defective title or a poorly drafted lease can outlive the deal itself. We handle sale and transfer of property, leases, documentation and due diligence with the care the asset deserves.
03 — Our Approach

The method behind the work.

01 — Direct Counsel

You deal with the advocate responsible for your matter from beginning to end. Continuity is not a courtesy — it is how strategy survives contact with the facts.

02 — Rigorous Preparation

Facts, documents and law are examined before positions are taken. Pleadings, affidavits, witness statements and supporting evidence are prepared to withstand the scrutiny they will face — reviewed before filing, not corrected after.

03 — Clear Advice

Legal advice that cannot be understood cannot be acted on. We state the issues, the options and the risks plainly — including the risks of proceeding, and of proceeding too late.

04 — Strategic Representation

Negotiation, correspondence, arbitration or litigation — the forum is chosen by the matter, and the strategy serves the client's lawful objective. We do not confuse activity with progress.
