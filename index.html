01 — The Firm

Deliberately sized to stay personal.

Wambogo & Company Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm operating from Hill Plaza on Upper Hill Close. The firm was built on a simple premise: clients are better served by advocates who handle their own files, know the facts cold, and are reachable directly — not routed through layers of associates.

That approach shows in how the firm works: commercial and employment matters are prepared with the same document discipline as litigation — witness statements, verifying affidavits, and evidence bundles built to withstand scrutiny, not just to be filed. Clients get a firm that treats paperwork as seriously as advocacy, because in Kenyan courts, it usually decides the case before anyone stands up to argue it.

03 — Approach
